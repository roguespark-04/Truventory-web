Why people keep a home inventory

Life gets busy. A quiet record of what you own — photos, serials, estimates — is useful long before you need it.

Not insurance. Truventory is a personal inventory record. It does not file insurance claims, contact insurers, partner with insurers, or guarantee that anyone will accept an export or pay a claim.

How it works

Five quiet steps from empty phone to a photo-filled inventory you control.

Walk a room

Name Living Room, Kitchen, Garage — whatever matches your home.

Built for quiet confidence

Local-first features that stay out of your way — and off the network.

Quick capture

Camera or photo library. Name, estimated value, notes, optional receipt photo.

Tap-to-pick serials

Photograph a plate; on-device OCR suggests numbers. Nothing uploads.

Rooms & totals

Estimated totals per room and for the whole house, always on this device.

Search that stays local

Find by name, serial, notes, or room — without a cloud index.

Free phone transfer

Export a .truventory backup with photos. Restore does not unlock Pro.

Pro PDF for your files

Save or share a photo-filled PDF through the system share sheet — you pick the destination.

Simple pricing

Start free. Upgrade when you need more items and PDF export.
