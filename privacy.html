Truventory Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026 · Last updated September 4, 2026

Truventory is a room-by-room home inventory app. This policy describes what the app does on your device. Truventory does not file insurance claims, contact insurers, or guarantee any payout.

Short version

  • Inventory stays on this phone. No account. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No ads. No analytics SDK in this version.
  • You may save a backup file locally or share it yourself.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the local inventory and photos.
  • PDF leaves the device when you Save or Share (Truventory Pro).

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Truventory mobile app (iOS and Android), bundle / application id com.truventory.truventory, published by the developer named on the App Store and Play listings. Contact that listing email for privacy questions. There is no Truventory account.

Data the app stores on your device

Truventory keeps a local inventory so it can work without a network:

  • Home name and optional address you type
  • Room names
  • Item names, estimated values, serial numbers, notes
  • File paths to item and receipt photos you attach
  • A local “unlocked” flag if you subscribe to or restore Truventory Pro

Photos you take or pick are copied into the app’s documents folder on that device. Search happens on-device. The developer of Truventory cannot see your rooms, items, photos, or address. Nothing is uploaded.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and this version of the app does not send to us:

  • Name, email, phone number, or account credentials (there is no account)
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, health, financial accounts, or government IDs
  • Analytics events, advertising IDs, or crash reports
  • Your photos, inventory backup, or PDF

Camera and photo-library access are optional. They are used to attach item and receipt pictures you choose, and to photograph a serial plate so the app can read the serial on this device. OCR never uploads the photo or the text. Truventory does not record audio. It does not browse the web.

In-app purchase

Truventory Pro (truventory_pro_yearly, $15 USD/year) is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple or Google. They process payment under their terms. Truventory stores only a local boolean that the 15-item cap is off and Save/Share PDF is enabled on that device. We do not receive your card number. Truventory Pro is not an insurance product. Cancel in your store account’s subscriptions settings.

Restore purchases asks the store whether this store account already has truventory_pro_yearly, then re-applies the local flag. Pro stays with the store account. Restoring a backup file does not grant Pro.

Sharing and exports

Nothing is shared with other users. There is no social graph.

If you tap Move to a new phone, Share backup, Save PDF, or Share PDF, a file you created is written to Downloads (Android) or the app documents folder (iOS), or sent through the share sheet to a destination you pick. Truventory does not transmit that file to us and does not file it as a claim. A .truventory backup includes photos so you can move to a new phone (older .byroom backups still restore); a PDF is for sharing, not restoring.

Children

Truventory is a general-audience utility (intended age rating 4+ / Everyone). It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information off the device.

Retention and deletion

Data lives on the device until you delete items, rooms, or the app. Uninstalling Truventory removes the local database and stored photos. There is no cloud copy.

Third parties

  • Apple and Google operate their app stores and in-app purchase systems.
  • The camera, photo picker, and share sheet are operating-system features.
  • Serial-number text recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device. The plate photo and recognized text are not sent to Google, to us, or to any other server for OCR.

This version does not embed advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Cloud

This version has no cloud backup and no account. Inventory stays on this phone. Transfer it with a backup file you save yourself.

Insurance disclaimer

Truventory is a personal inventory record. It does not file insurance claims, does not partner with insurers, and does not guarantee that an insurer will accept the export or pay a claim.

Contact

Use the support email on the App Store or Play listing for Truventory. There is no in-app inbox.
